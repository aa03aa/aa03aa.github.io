Privacy Policy

Mosaic Journey: Block Puzzle · Last updated: 2026-09-26

This policy explains what information the game "Mosaic Journey: Block Puzzle" (the "Game") collects, why, and the choices you have. The Game does not require an account and does not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

1. Data stored only on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, scores, coins, settings, the unfinished game) is saved locally on your phone. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the Game or using "Reset progress" in Settings deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process: the device advertising ID, device and app information (model, OS version, language), IP address (used to estimate a general location such as country), and ad interactions. Depending on your region and choice, ads may be personalised or non-personalised.

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in US states with privacy laws, a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) asks for your choice when required.
  • You can change your choice at any time: Settings → Privacy & ad preferences.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Google → Ads).

More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

If enabled, Firebase Analytics collects anonymous usage events (for example which level was started or finished, app version, device type, country) to help us improve the Game. Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports (device state and technical details at the time of the crash). These do not include your name or contact details. Firebase Remote Config downloads game settings (such as difficulty) and does not collect personal data.

4. Sharing

When you tap "Share", an image of your result is created on your device and passed to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp or Instagram). We do not receive it.

5. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Data retention and security

Analytics and crash data are kept by Google according to Google's retention settings (up to 14 months for analytics events by default). Data is transferred using encryption (HTTPS).

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state laws) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data. Because we do not hold data that identifies you, most requests can be handled directly on your device (Privacy & ad preferences, advertising ID reset, uninstalling). For anything else, contact us below.
