Moonhollow - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains how YUSAME ("we") handles information in the mobile game Moonhollow ("the game").

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name or email address. Game progress (coins, upgrades, settings) is stored only on your device. If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, Google shares your Play Games player name and player ID with the game so it can show that you are signed in, and your game progress is backed up with Google Play Games Saved Games in your own Google account so it can be restored on a new device. Signing in is optional. Scores you reach may appear on Google Play Games leaderboards under your Play Games name.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device information and ad interaction data. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required by law, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options inside the game, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data. If you signed in with Google Play Games, a copy is kept in your Google account; you can delete it in the Google Play Games app (Settings > Delete Play Games account and data, or the game's data). We do not keep copies on our own servers.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
